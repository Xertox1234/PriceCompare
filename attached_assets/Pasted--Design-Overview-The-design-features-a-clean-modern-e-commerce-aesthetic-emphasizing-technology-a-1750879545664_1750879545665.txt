🎨 Design Overview
The design features a clean, modern e-commerce aesthetic emphasizing technology and gadgets. It leverages ample white space, contrasting elements, and bold imagery to attract attention.

📐 Layout & Structure
Grid Layout:
Consistent use of CSS Grid and Flexbox.

Prominent Hero banner at the top.

Clear categorization with distinct sections.

Main Sections:
Hero Section: Large promotional banner with CTA.

Featured Categories: Horizontal block cards.

Category Listings: Icons with text below.

Product Showcase Grid: Asymmetric layout for visual interest.

Trending Products Section: Dual-columns of products.

Promotional Banners: Full-width visual banners with CTAs.

Informational Section: Simple steps and service explanations.

Footer: Multi-column layout with quick links and newsletter signup.

🌈 Color Scheme
Primary Colors:

Vibrant blue (#5A5DFF) for accents and buttons.

Deep black (#000000) and pure white (#FFFFFF) for text and backgrounds.

Secondary Colors:

Shades of grey (#f7f7f7, #eaeaea) for subtle backgrounds and dividers.

🖋️ Typography
Primary Font: Modern sans-serif (Inter or Montserrat recommended)

Heading: Bold, sans-serif (e.g., weight 700)

Subheadings: Medium weight (e.g., 500–600)

Body Text: Regular weight (e.g., 400)

Sizes & Spacing:

Headings: 28–48px

Subheadings: 20–24px

Body Text: 14–16px

CTA Buttons: Bold text, approx 16px

🖼️ Imagery
Crisp, high-resolution photos on white or subtle gradients.

Product-focused, minimalistic style with significant negative space.

🔘 Interactive Elements & Buttons
Buttons:

Primary: Vibrant blue background (#5A5DFF), white text, rounded corners (~4px).

Secondary: White background, dark grey border, and text.

Hover effects: Slight darkening or elevation effect.

📱 Responsiveness
Breakpoints:

Mobile (<768px): Stack all columns vertically.

Tablet (768px–1024px): Two-column grid adjustments.

Desktop (>1024px): Max-width containers, centered layout.

⚙️ CSS Techniques & Animations
Smooth CSS transitions on hover (buttons, links, images).

Subtle fade-in animations for main sections as they enter the viewport.
